# Homepage hero visuals

Generated with the built-in image generation tool, with transparent backgrounds enabled. These are decorative category inspiration assets; catalog product images and records were not changed.

## Project assets

- `public/hero-cake-cutout.png`: full-resolution PNG source with alpha.
- `public/hero-kitchen-cutout.png`: full-resolution PNG source with alpha.
- `public/hero-cake-cutout.webp`: 1200 x 1200, alpha preserved, 229308 bytes.
- `public/hero-kitchen-cutout.webp`: 720 x 720, alpha preserved, 86606 bytes.

The homepage serves WebP derivatives through responsive Next Image resizing. Both images use object-contain to keep the full subject visible. PNG sources and previous hero files remain available.

## Cake generation prompt

Use case: ads-marketing. Create a premium photorealistic bakery hero asset for Bake Bazaar Mart, a warm cream, navy and orange ecommerce website. A truly transparent PNG background, no backdrop, no rectangle, no text, no branding. One irresistible elegant celebration cake, creamy ivory buttercream with delicate piping, fresh strawberries and raspberries, tiny pistachio accents; a beautifully cut slice beside it exposing moist vanilla sponge and berry filling. On a minimal pale ceramic cake stand with a couple of loose berries near base. Sophisticated artisanal bakery photography, soft natural studio illumination, realistic edible textures, appetizing not plastic. Three-quarter front view, entire cake and stand visible, centered balanced compact composition occupying 85 percent of a square canvas, generous transparent margin all around so nothing clips on responsive mobile layouts. Soft subtle contact shadow only, alpha everywhere else. No candles, no hands, no utensils, no people, no stock-photo kitchen. This is decorative cake inspiration rather than a specific catalog product.

## Kitchen generation prompt

Use case: ads-marketing. Create a premium photorealistic transparent PNG cutout for the kitchen essentials card of Bake Bazaar Mart, matching warm ivory bakery photography and a cream/navy/orange website. A tasteful compact arrangement of everyday baking and kitchen tools: ivory ceramic mixing bowl in front, warm beechwood rolling pin diagonally beside it, a stainless-steel whisk and two muted terracotta/orange silicone spatulas resting in a small ivory utensil pot, neatly stacked small measuring cups. Beautiful realistic materials, natural studio light, soft delicate contact shadow. Front three-quarter camera, square composition, every object fully visible inside canvas, about 12 percent transparent margin, clear silhouette readable even at small mobile card sizes. Completely transparent background/alpha, no tabletop rectangle, no kitchen room, no text, no logos, no watermark, no food, no people. Elegant inviting ecommerce editorial asset, not a catalog SKU depiction.
